What we do

Whatever your model needs to learn, we collect or label it with trained people and clear guidelines.

Voice recording

Scripted and free speech from real speakers, recorded to your specs: devices, environments, ages, dialects.

Audio labeling

We mark who is speaking, what the sound is (speech, music, noise, alarm) and when it starts and ends.

Transcription

Accurate text for speech in Arabic dialects and other languages, with timestamps and speaker tags.

Quality review

Every batch is checked by a second reviewer and measured against your acceptance score before delivery.

Common questions

Can we start with a small pilot?

Yes. We recommend it. Send your guidelines and we deliver a small sample for approval before the full project starts.

What do we need to send you?

A short description of the goal, your labeling or recording guidelines, the languages needed, the volume, and your deadline. Sample files help a lot.

How is the data delivered?

In the format you ask for, such as audio files with JSON or CSV labels. We agree on the format during the brief.

Do you sign an NDA?

Yes. Tell us before you share data and we will sign yours or provide ours.

Tell us about your project

Send a few details and we will reply with a plan and a quote.
